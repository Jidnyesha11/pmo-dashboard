# Sprint Setup

`data/sprints.csv` contains the full baseline, thirteen 14-day sprint periods for the six-month lifecycle. The initial portfolio dashboard focuses on SPRINT-01 through SPRINT-06. The reporting date is in SPRINT-08, so the demonstration window and current sprint are deliberately distinct. Import sprint names, goals and start/end dates into Jira; map stories to the configured sprint IDs in `Jira_Import_Data.csv`. Sprint velocity is the sum of completed story points only. Keep carryover visible and do not count partially completed points as done.
