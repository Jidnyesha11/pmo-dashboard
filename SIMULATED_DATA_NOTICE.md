# Simulated Data Notice

This complete portfolio environment is a fictional planning simulation. Northstar Retail is fictional. Names, roles, work hours, cost rates, task dates, budget transactions, customer outcomes, quality measures, defect counts, and governance records are generated for demonstration and are not evidence of actual employee performance, delivery outcomes, financial results, model capability, or customer activity.

AI quality observations use a simple synthetic monthly prompt sample. They illustrate numerator/denominator handling and report design only. They do not constitute a benchmark, safety certification, or deployment evaluation. Cost values illustrate a budget model; they are not accounting statements. Dates and dependencies illustrate a baseline; they are not commitments.

Do not use this data to make operational, hiring, financial, customer, or model-selection decisions. Replace it with authorized, quality-controlled data and appropriate controls before operational use.
