# AI Support Platform PMO Home

## Purpose

Govern a six-month simulated Scrum delivery for an AI customer-support platform that answers common questions from approved documents, cites sources, creates tickets, escalates uncertain requests, and reports service and AI quality.

## Current reporting snapshot

- Reporting date: 17 April 2026; weekly delivery review.
- Baseline: 5 January–3 July 2026; 13 two-week sprints; initial dashboard window: six sprints.
- Approved budget: ₹25,00,000 including ₹4,00,000 contingency.
- Key gates: approved sources, retrieval MVP, integrated test exit, UAT sign-off, production go-live.
- Attention: retrieval grounding, temporary QA capacity, CRM integration, source approval.

## Linked databases

Tasks, Risks, Decisions, Stakeholders, Meetings, Milestones, Epics, Stories, Sprints, Costs, Resources. All imported records are simulated. See `DATA_DICTIONARY.md` for field/relationship definitions.

## Operating rhythm

Weekly delivery review for progress, blockers, forecast and risk triggers. Monthly steering committee for scope, budget, material risks, and gate decisions. Sprint review and retrospective at each 14-day boundary.
