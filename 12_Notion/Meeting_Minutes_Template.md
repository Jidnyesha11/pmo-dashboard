## Template variants to create from this page

- **Steering committee:** emphasize forecast, baseline changes, critical risks, gate decisions, and sponsor approvals.
- **Sprint review:** add sprint goal, accepted story IDs, demo links, carryover, stakeholder feedback, and backlog decisions.
- **Sprint retrospective:** use Start / Stop / Continue, then capture action owner, due date, and next-sprint check.
- **Weekly delivery review:** retain the status table, overdue tasks, dependencies, actual/forecast cost, capacity, and escalation requests.
